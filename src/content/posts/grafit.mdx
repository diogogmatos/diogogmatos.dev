### Origins

It all started with a **class** we collectively took in the final year of our Software Engineering Master's degree. The class, focused on **Entrepreneurship and Innovation**, challenged us to come up with a startup idea and develop a prototype. The idea of an app that could help people manage their wardrobe and share outfit inspiration had been floating around **Carlos's** mind for a while, and the class provided the perfect opportunity to bring it to life. We formed a team, combining our skills in software development, design, and business strategy, and set out to create something fun.

![](/assets/grafit/bootcamp.webp)

Although it started as a *just-for-fun* project, the university's partnership with Startup Braga's [Startup Point](https://startuppoint.startupbraga.com/) competition quickly made us take it to the next level. The competition provided its **12 teams** with *mentorship*, *resources*, *exposure* to industry experts, and six *bootcamps* where we learned everything from product strategy and business design to pitching and growth strategy.

### The Graph

From the start, we wanted Grafit's navigation to be original and set itself apart from the tired concept of a grid. We envisioned a **graph-based navigation system** that would allow users to discover outfits in a more dynamic and engaging way, by exploring in **any direction** through an **infinite canvas**. The metadata behind each outfit would allow to group outfits with a similar *vibe*, allowing users to organically discover new styles by moving further away from their starting point into different niches.

<ContainedImage src={["/assets/grafit/explore.webp"]} alt="Grafit's graph-based navigation system" addPadding />

By joining the concepts of **Graph** and Out<em>**fit**</em>, this distinctive idea was what gave name to the app: **Grafit**.

### The Outfit

We wanted the outfit view to be **immersive**, putting the outfit picture front and center, rather than a collage of clothing items like most competing apps. The background of each outfit is identified upon submission, allowing the view to cleanly focus on the subject.

<ContainedImage src={["/assets/grafit/outfit-open.webp", "/assets/grafit/outfit-b.webp"]} alt="Grafit's outfit view" addPadding />

A swipe up reveals further details such as *season*, *weather*, number of *wears*, *tags* and additional shots of the outfit. A swipe right moves to the wardrobe view, where further details about each item can be explored by simply tapping on it. Here, the user can also edit the details of each item or add those that might have been missed by the AI-recognition system.
